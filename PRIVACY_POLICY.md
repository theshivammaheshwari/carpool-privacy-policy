# Privacy Policy for Carpool App

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026  

Welcome to **Carpool App** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application ("Carpool App" or the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Carpool App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not use the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide you with seamless carpool management and shared expense tracking.

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Information:** When you sign up or log in (via Email/Password or Google Sign-In), we collect your name, email address, and profile photo URL.
- **Vehicle Information (Optional for Car Owners):** If you register a vehicle to log carpool trips, we collect the vehicle model/name and license plate number. This allows other carpool members to recognize the vehicle.
- **Carpool & Expense Data:** Information regarding your carpool groups, logged trips (date, distance, driver, passengers), fuel expenses (petrol rate, credits applied), and settlement payment records.

### B. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Financial Credentials:** We **do not** collect or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account passwords, or UPI PINs. Any monetary transactions between members occur outside the app (peer-to-peer), and the app merely acts as a calculation ledger.
- **No Continuous Background Location:** We **do not** track your continuous background GPS location. 
- **No Access to Contacts or SMS:** We do not read your private contacts, messages, or phone storage.

### C. Automatically Collected Information
- **App Usage & Device Information:** Basic operational data such as device model, operating system version, and crash diagnostic logs to maintain stability and app security.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:
1. **Account Creation & Authentication:** To authenticate your identity and secure your account using Firebase Authentication.
2. **Carpool Group Operations:** To allow you to create, join, and manage private carpool groups via invite codes.
3. **Expense Calculations:** To calculate fair distance and fuel expense splits, show net balances, and suggest optimal settlement transactions among group members.
4. **App Improvement & Security:** To prevent fraudulent group joining and maintain application performance.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We integrate with trusted third-party service providers to power essential features:
- **Google Play Services:** For core app infrastructure on Android devices.
- **Firebase Authentication (Google LLC):** For secure user registration and login management.
- **Google Cloud Firestore (Google LLC):** For cloud database storage with security rules restricting data access only to authorized group members.

These third parties process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

Your data is only shared in the following circumstances:
- **Within Your Carpool Groups:** Your display name, profile avatar, vehicle details (if driver), and trip logs are visible exclusively to other verified members of the groups you explicitly join.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, subpoena, or regulation to protect our legal rights.

## 5. Account and Data Deletion (User Rights)

You have full control over your personal data.

### How to Delete Your Account:
1. Open **Carpool App**.
2. Go to **Profile** (top right profile icon).
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm the deletion prompt in the dialog box.

### What Happens When You Delete Your Account:
- Your Firebase Authentication user record will be permanently deleted.
- Your personal profile record in Firestore will be removed.
- You will be immediately signed out of the application.

### Deletion Requests via Email:
If you are unable to access the app or wish to request data deletion externally, you can email us directly at:  
**Email:** `shivam.developer.contact@gmail.com` *(or your registered developer email)*  
Include your registered email address with the subject line: *"Request Account and Data Deletion - Carpool App"*. We will process and confirm your request within 7 business days.
